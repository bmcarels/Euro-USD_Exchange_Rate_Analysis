**Project Summary:**
This project is an analysis of how the Euro-USD exchange rate changed over time and what factors may have influenced that change. The goal of this project was to create “explanatory” graphs; graphs that convey a clear message to the audience. The first graph explores the change in the Euro-USD exchange rate during the 2008 US financial crisis, and the second graph explores the change in the Euro-USD exchange rate during the three presidencies from the year 2000-2021.

**Key Findings:**
The Euro-USD exchange rate was the highest between the years 2000-2021 when it was valued at 1.599 in 2008.
The greatest fluctuations in the exchange rate occurred during George W. Bush's presidency (2001-2009).

**Datasource:**
The data for this project is provided by the European Central Bank and can be found on Kaggle. The dataset details the euro daily exchange rates from 1999 to 2021. There are 5,699 rows and 41 columns in the dataset. Of those 41 columns, 40 are unique countries.
